Space Defense

How to Play?

Defend Earth from alien invasion by placing defensive buildings on the grid. Use energy to build structures that block enemy movement and fire bullets. Prevent enemies from reaching the bottom of the screen while managing your resources. Survive all enemy waves to win!

This game is a modified example from the HTML5 Game Development Hotshot book by Makzan. Free for personal and commerical use.
