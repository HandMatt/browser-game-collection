# Space Defenders

An exciting tower defense game set in space! Defend Earth from alien invasion by building an economy and deploying attack units in a strategic grid-based defense system. Enemies descend from the top of the screen, and you must protect Earth at the bottom by placing defense buildings that block enemy movement and fire bullets to eliminate threats. Survive the time limit and eliminate all invaders before they reach our planet!

## Quick Start

To run the game locally, use Python's built-in HTTP server:

```bash
python3 -m http.server 8000
```

Then open your browser and navigate to:

```
http://localhost:8000
```

## How to Play

1. **Build Your Economy**: Generate resources to fund your defense operations
2. **Strategic Placement**: Place defense buildings on the grid to block enemy paths
3. **Combat Units**: Deploy attack units that fire bullets to eliminate invaders
4. **Protect Earth**: Prevent enemies from reaching the bottom of the screen
5. **Survive the Clock**: Eliminate all enemies within the time limit to win

**Strategy**: Think tactically! Build your economy efficiently, position your defenses to create optimal chokepoints, and manage your attack units to maximize damage output. Enemies will attack anything blocking their path to Earth, so balance your defensive and offensive capabilities to survive the invasion!

## Game Mechanics

- **Grid-Based System**: Space is divided into a grid where you place buildings
- **Enemy Movement**: Invaders descend from top to bottom, targeting Earth
- **Building Types**: Some buildings block movement, others fire bullets at enemies
- **Resource Management**: Build and maintain your economy to fund defenses
- **Time Pressure**: Complete objectives within strict time limits
