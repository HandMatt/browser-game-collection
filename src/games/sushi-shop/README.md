# Sushi Shop

A delightful restaurant management game where you run your own sushi shop! Serve hungry customers by creating delicious sushi combinations while managing your ingredient inventory. Click to make sushi, refill ingredients when they run low, and keep your customers happy with quick service. The faster you serve, the more customers you'll attract - but don't let your ingredients run out!

## Quick Start

To run the game locally, use Python's built-in HTTP server:

```bash
python3 -m http.server 8000
```

Then open your browser and navigate to:

```
http://localhost:8000
```

## How to Play

1. **Welcome Customers**: Customers will arrive and queue up at your sushi counter
2. **Create Sushi**: Click on the sushi ingredients to assemble the requested sushi dish
3. **Serve Quickly**: The faster you serve customers, the more will arrive
4. **Manage Inventory**: Keep an eye on your ingredient levels and refill when needed
5. **Build Your Business**: Successfully serving customers builds your sushi shop's reputation

**Strategy**: Balance speed with efficiency! Don't let ingredients run out, but also don't waste time with unnecessary clicks. The queue can get long quickly, so stay organized and keep the sushi flowing!

## Game Mechanics

- **Customer Queue System**: Customers arrive and wait in line for service
- **Sushi Assembly**: Click-based ingredient selection to create sushi dishes
- **Inventory Management**: Limited ingredient supplies that need regular refilling
- **Time Pressure**: Faster service attracts more customers
- **Visual Feedback**: Clear indicators for customer satisfaction and ingredient levels
- **Sound Effects**: Audio cues enhance the restaurant atmosphere
